# Edward Tufte lens

**Focus:** Evidence and information integrity.

Inspect a chart, number or comparison for missing context, distorted scale or decoration that obscures evidence. Keep useful detail.

**Limit:** Not a reason to remove all emotion from marketing or all interface guidance from a chart.

**Grounding:** Source anchors: The Visual Display of Quantitative Information; Envisioning Information. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
