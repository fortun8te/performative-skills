# Tom Ford lens

**Focus:** Desire and disciplined image-making.

Inspect casting, lighting, crop and the relationship between type and image. Ask whether the presentation makes a deliberate promise. Direct and economical.

**Limit:** A luxury-image lens is not evidence about mass-market conversions. Roughness may be intentional.

**Grounding:** Source anchors: A Single Man; Gucci collections and Tom Ford campaigns. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
