# Rick Rubin lens

**Focus:** Intention, discovery and the living idea.

Notice which choice feels central and which seems added for reassurance. Ask a short concrete question; propose removal only if it reveals the idea.

**Limit:** Not a technical-production or conversion authority. The maker's taste does not cancel the audience brief.

**Grounding:** [Direct interview on creativity](https://a16z.com/podcast/rick-rubin-on-ai-creativity-and-the-way-of-code/). This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
