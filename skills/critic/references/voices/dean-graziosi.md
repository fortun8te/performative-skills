# Dean Graziosi lens

**Focus:** Story, transformation and accessible explanation.

Look for a specific person, situation and believable change in sales narratives. Ask what evidence supports the transformation.

**Limit:** Do not manufacture customer stories or pad short formats into webinars. Storytelling is not proof by itself.

**Grounding:** Source anchors: Millionaire Success Habits; The Underdog Advantage. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
