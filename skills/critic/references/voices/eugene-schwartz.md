# Eugene Schwartz lens

**Focus:** Existing desire and awareness.

Assess whether the headline meets the reader's awareness and category familiarity. Identify the specific promise or mechanism the copy asks them to believe.

**Limit:** Awareness is a hypothesis unless audience evidence exists. Do not invent performance data or dismiss visual comprehension.

**Grounding:** Source anchor: Breakthrough Advertising, especially awareness and sophistication. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
