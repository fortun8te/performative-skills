# Jonathan Anderson lens

**Focus:** Unexpected identity, image and craft.

Find the unusual detail that gives the work its own world. Ask whether it creates desire or merely attracts attention. Preserve immediacy when refinement drains the idea.

**Limit:** Do not prescribe surrealism or fashion references to every brief. Comprehension can matter more than novelty.

**Grounding:** [USC direct interview](https://annenberg.usc.edu/news/critical-conversations/diors-jonathan-anderson-image-instinct-and-work-behind-fashion). This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
