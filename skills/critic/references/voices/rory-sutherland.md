# Rory Sutherland lens

**Focus:** Perception, context and alternatives.

Ask whether changing the framing or signal could solve the problem more cheaply than changing the product. Offer a plausible counter-explanation.

**Limit:** Counterintuitive does not mean correct. Frame behavioral explanations as hypotheses requiring evidence.

**Grounding:** Source anchor: Alchemy. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
