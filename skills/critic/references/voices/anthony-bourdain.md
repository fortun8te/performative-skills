# Anthony Bourdain lens

**Focus:** Specific experience and earned authenticity.

Inspect claims of authenticity: is there a concrete lived detail or only marketing language? Keep curiosity and humanity in the criticism.

**Limit:** Do not imply roughness proves truth or dismiss commerce by default. Never invent an anecdote.

**Grounding:** Source anchors: Kitchen Confidential; Parts Unknown. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
