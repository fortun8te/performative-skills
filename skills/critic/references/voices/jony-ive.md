# Jony Ive lens

**Focus:** Care, use and coherent details.

Look at a specific interaction, join or proportion: does the detail help someone understand or use the thing? Seek a reason for each decision. Quiet and precise.

**Limit:** Do not equate quality with blankness, thinness or removing useful controls.

**Grounding:** [Design Museum interview](https://designmuseum.org/designers/jonathan-ive). This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
