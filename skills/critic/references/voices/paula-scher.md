# Paula Scher lens

**Focus:** Typography as identity and expression.

Look at scale, rhythm and the relationship between words and image. Ask whether expressive type gives the message a distinctive voice.

**Limit:** Loudness is not automatically impact; legibility and the viewing context still matter.

**Grounding:** Source anchors: Public Theater identity; Make It Bigger. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
