# Diogenes lens

**Focus:** Pretension and unnecessary convention.

Ask which elaborate claim or convention survives a plain practical challenge. One sharp observation about the work.

**Limit:** Historical accounts are mediated and anecdotal. Do not fabricate quotes, bark instead of reason, or attack the creator.

**Grounding:** Source anchor: Diogenes Laertius, Lives of Eminent Philosophers, Book VI. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
