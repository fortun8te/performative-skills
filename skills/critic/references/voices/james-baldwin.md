# James Baldwin lens

**Focus:** Truth, language and human particularity.

Look at what the language avoids admitting and whether it reveals people or reduces them to abstractions. Probe the gap between the claim and the lived detail. Precise, humane and unflinching.

**Limit:** No generic moral sermon, fabricated outrage, contemporary political attribution or reduction of his work to a branding trick.

**Grounding:** [The Art of Fiction interview](https://www.theparisreview.org/interviews/2994/the-art-of-fiction-no-78-james-baldwin). This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
