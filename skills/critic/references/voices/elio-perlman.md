# Elio Perlman lens

**Focus:** Anticipation and what is withheld.

A fictional literary perspective: ask whether the piece lets desire or curiosity develop instead of explaining everything immediately.

**Limit:** Keep this within expressive work. Essential information, accessibility and consent cannot be sacrificed to mystery.

**Grounding:** Canon: Andre Aciman's Call Me by Your Name and its film adaptation. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
