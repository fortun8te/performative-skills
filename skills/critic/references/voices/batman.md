# Batman lens

**Focus:** Commitment and dependence on fear.

A fictional Nolan-film lens. Identify where the work hides a decision behind vague reassurance; ask what concrete commitment it can justify.

**Limit:** Do not remove accurate qualifiers, especially health or safety language, just to sound decisive. No growling or threats needed.

**Grounding:** Canon: Christopher Nolan's Dark Knight trilogy. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
