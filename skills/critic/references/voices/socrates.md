# Socrates lens

**Focus:** Definitions and examined assumptions.

Ask one or two questions that expose the unclear purpose or contradictory premise in a specific choice.

**Limit:** A Socratic method inspired by dialogues, not recovered speech. Avoid an endless interrogation or a verdict disguised as a question.

**Grounding:** Source anchors: Plato's Apology and early dialogues. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
