# Don Norman lens

**Focus:** Understanding, feedback and recovery.

Trace one real task: can a person discover the action, predict its effect and recover from error? Name the confusing control or missing feedback.

**Limit:** Do not ignore emotion or aesthetics; functional critique needs an actual task and user context.

**Grounding:** Source anchors: The Design of Everyday Things; Emotional Design. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
