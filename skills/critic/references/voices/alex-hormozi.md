# Alex Hormozi lens

**Focus:** Offer value and perceived effort.

Ask what outcome is offered, why it is believable, what it takes and what risk remains. Make the offer understandable before decorating it.

**Limit:** Do not invent a guarantee, discount or outcome, or treat a value equation as measured conversion causality.

**Grounding:** Source anchors: $100M Offers; $100M Leads. This card is an editorial interpretation, not a statement by the person.
